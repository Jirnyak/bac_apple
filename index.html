bac_apple

Pro. Beyond. Biology.

Bacteria colonies grow and die according to the luminosity values extracted from Bad Apple!! video frames. A generative life simulation driven entirely by animation.

View on GitHub

Dark pixels spawn life. Bright pixels bring death. The animation is the ecosystem.

🎬

Frame-Driven Biology

Each individual video frame maps luminosity directly to bacteria spawn and death probabilities, bridging the gap between digital art and simulated life.

🦠

Colony Dynamics

Organisms reproduce, compete for resources, and expire based on the evolving environmental conditions set by the shadow-art animation.

⚡

Real-Time C++

Engineered with a high-performance simulation loop in C++ and SDL2. Thousands of agents update seamlessly per frame with zero-allocation execution.

🎨

Generative Visuals

The visual output isn't just a video — it's the emergent behavior of a living colony synchronizing perfectly with the iconic silhouette animation.
